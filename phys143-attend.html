Attendance check-in

How your data is used

Purpose: to note that you came to this class.

What is kept: your student ID, the date and time, whether you were within range of the classroom (a distance in metres, not where you are), and a scrambled code for your phone, used only to stop one phone checking in several students. A random code is stored in your phone's browser for this. Your location is used once and is not stored.

Who sees it: the lecturer only. Student IDs are not shown publicly.

How long: the phone codes and any flags are deleted at the end of the semester. The check-in is kept in my class sheet.

Questions or corrections: hdonglo@ug.edu.gh. This follows the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

Your phone may ask for your location. It is used once to confirm you are in the room; only the distance is kept, not where you are.
