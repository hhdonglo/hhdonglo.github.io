Teaching

Teaching is an integral component of my academic work at the University of Ghana. My teaching integrates foundational physics, quantitative reasoning, experimental practice, and scientific computing, with an emphasis on connecting classroom concepts to contemporary research in nuclear physics and computational modelling.

Teaching Philosophy

My teaching emphasizes:

  • Conceptual understanding before mathematical formalism
  • Quantitative problem-solving and physical modelling
  • Experimental reasoning, measurement, and uncertainty analysis
  • Computational approaches to solving physical problems
  • Connections between foundational physics and modern research
  • Developing independent scientific thinking and communication skills

Teaching in Context

My teaching complements my broader research programme in nuclear reaction dynamics, uncertainty quantification, scientific computing, and predictive modelling. Through this integration, students are exposed not only to the foundations of physics but also to the methods used in contemporary research.
