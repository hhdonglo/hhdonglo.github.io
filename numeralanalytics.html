NumeralAnalytics GH

Coming soon

Physics-informed analytics for physical industries

An upcoming project that brings physical modelling and uncertainty-aware data science to real physical systems, where data are sparse and the cost of a wrong estimate is high. The aim is analysis whose confidence is stated as clearly as its result.

  • Anomaly detection
  • Model calibration
  • Surrogate modelling
  • Data assimilation
  • Experimental design
  • Risk-informed decisions

NumeralAnalytics GH is in preparation. Details will be announced here. For enquiries, see Contact.
