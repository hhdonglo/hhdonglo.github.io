This term at a glance

Students who may need attention

Rostered students below either threshold, who missed a live quiz, or with no practice recorded in the period. Practice counts only when a student entered an ID on the practice page, so "no practice recorded" is a prompt for a conversation, not evidence.

Lectures by proportion correct

Every answer recorded for the lecture's questions: live quizzes, practice and the mock exam.

Questions to look at first

The ten questions with the lowest proportion correct among those with at least five answers. A low proportion with a strong most-chosen wrong option points to a shared misconception; a discrimination below 0.2 means stronger and weaker students did about equally well, so the wording or the key may need checking. "Confident and wrong" counts students who rated their confidence high and answered wrongly in a live quiz.
