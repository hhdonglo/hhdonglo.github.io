Student groups


Make groups

Groups are drawn from the class list. Among many possible groupings, the one that repeats the fewest pairings from earlier sets is kept. Locked groups stay as they are when you make a set again.





Mixed by quiz results gives each group a spread of stronger and weaker students, from the released live quiz results; students without results are placed in the middle. Mixed by attendance does the same with attendance rates.


Control Centre

Group marks are copied to every member: in the Groups tab, and in Master and Scores as a column "Group: name of the set" (not counted in the live quiz total). Students see their group, its members and the mark in the Portal under My progress.
