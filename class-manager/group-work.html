Group work

Sign in with your Student ID and the personal access code your lecturer gave you. You see only your own groups' assignments.



The group submits one shared answer; the latest version counts, and every member can update it until the assignment closes. Each member also says what they did. Ratings of your group-mates are seen only by your lecturer, to understand how groups work; they never change anyone's mark on their own.
