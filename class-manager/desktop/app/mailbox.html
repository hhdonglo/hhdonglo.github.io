What the mailbox does

Practice results and question reports are saved on each student's phone and sent to CM. If CM is off or out of reach, the phone leaves them, encrypted, in your mailbox. CM collects them the next time it is open. The mailbox cannot read them, holds no names, and forgets everything after 14 days. Check-in and live quizzes never use it: they need CM in the room.

Optional. Without a mailbox, phones keep each result and send it as soon as CM can be reached.

Set up

The steps for creating the mailbox (about ten minutes, free) are in class-manager/relay/README.md. They give you an address and two codes.
