Attendance check-in

Your ID and code are saved on this phone. Press Check in.

How your data is used

Purpose: to note that you came to this class.

What is kept: your student ID, the date and time, whether you were within range of the classroom (a distance in metres, not where you are), and a scrambled code for your phone. The first phone you check in with is registered to you for the term, so that no one else can check in with it and no one can check in for you from another phone. A random code is stored in your phone's browser for this. Your location is used once and is not stored.

Using another phone: if your phone is not working, you may check in on a classmate's phone with your own ID and access code. The check-in waits until your lecturer confirms it.

Who sees it: the lecturer only. Student IDs are not shown publicly.

How long: the phone codes, registered phones and any flags are deleted at the end of the semester. The check-in is kept in the lecturer's class records.

Questions or corrections: ask the lecturer.

Your phone may ask for your location. It is used once to confirm you are in the room; only the distance is kept, not where you are.
