Lecturer login

Reset all results (clean slate)

Empties attendance and quiz results so the term starts fresh. Every tab it clears is copied to a backup tab (Backup_<tab>_<date_time>) first, and a copy of the whole Sheet is saved in your Drive too. Nothing is cleared if a tab backup cannot be checked. The Roster, join codes, host key, quiz settings, question banks and the script are not touched. Attendance is refilled with the Student IDs on the Roster.
