Theoretical & Computational Nuclear Physics

Nuclear Reaction Dynamics · Superheavy-Element Synthesis · Computational Modelling · Uncertainty Quantification

Research on how superheavy nuclei form in heavy-ion collisions, using stochastic reaction models, model calibration and uncertainty quantification. The same computational methods extend into scientific computing, machine learning and data engineering.

Research

The central question: which reaction mechanisms govern the formation of superheavy nuclei, and how reliably can they be predicted?

  • How does a touching nuclear system fuse into a compound nucleus, rather than re-separate?
  • How well can cross sections for new elements such as Z = 119 and 120 be predicted?
  • Which model parameters do experimental data actually constrain, and with what uncertainty?
