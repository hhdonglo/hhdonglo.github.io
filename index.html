I am a theoretical nuclear physicist and Lecturer in Physics at the University of Ghana. My research focuses on nuclear reaction dynamics, the synthesis of superheavy elements, scientific computing, and uncertainty quantification.

Alongside my research, I develop computational and data-driven methods for scientific applications, with interests in data science, machine learning, and data engineering.

Research

Superheavy-Element Synthesis

I study the reaction mechanisms governing the formation of superheavy nuclei, with particular interest in fusion dynamics, formation probabilities, survival probabilities, and theoretical modelling of fusion–evaporation reactions.

Explore Research →

Nuclear Reaction Modelling

Development and application of theoretical models and computational methods for describing heavy-ion reactions and the formation of heavy and superheavy nuclei.

Explore Research →

Uncertainty Quantification

Investigation of model sensitivity, parameter variation, calibration, and uncertainty propagation in nuclear-reaction calculations.

Explore Research →

Scientific Computing, Data Science and Engineering

I apply computational and data-driven methods to scientific problems, with interests spanning scientific software, high-performance computing, data analysis, machine learning, and reproducible computational workflows.
