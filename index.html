Welcome

My research investigates the formation of superheavy nuclei in heavy-ion collisions and the fundamental limits of nuclear stability. Through reaction modelling, uncertainty quantification and scientific computing, I develop predictive frameworks for experiments searching for new elements and exploring the limits of the periodic table.

Alongside nuclear physics, I develop computational methods for scientific modelling, data-intensive computing and machine learning applications in physics.

Research Vision

To develop predictive theories of nuclear reactions that guide the discovery of new elements and reveal the ultimate limits of the periodic table.

Scientific Questions

  • How heavy can matter become?
  • Where does the periodic table end?
  • Can long-lived superheavy nuclei exist?
  • How predictive are our theories at the limits of nuclear stability?

Research Highlights

  • Reaction dynamics of superheavy-element synthesis
  • Fusion, quasifission and survival mechanisms in heavy-ion collisions
  • Calibration and uncertainty quantification of nuclear reaction models
  • Scientific computing, machine learning and data-intensive methods for physics
