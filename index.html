Hope Donglo

Lecturer in Physics · University of Ghana

Theoretical nuclear physicist working on the reaction mechanisms that govern the formation of superheavy nuclei, with stochastic dynamics and uncertainty quantification as the principal methods. Teaches undergraduate physics, including PHYS 143, and leads laboratory sessions.
