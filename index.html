Research on the reaction mechanisms behind the synthesis of superheavy elements, using stochastic modelling, scientific computing and uncertainty quantification, with applications in machine learning and data engineering.

Research

Nuclear Reaction Dynamics

I study the dynamics of heavy-ion reactions, with particular interest in fusion–evaporation reactions and the synthesis of superheavy elements: capture, compound-nucleus formation, survival, and their theoretical modelling.

Uncertainty Quantification
