About

Hope Donglo, PhD, is a theoretical nuclear physicist and Lecturer in Physics at the University of Ghana.

His research develops theoretical and computational models of heavy-ion reactions and superheavy element synthesis, using stochastic dynamics, uncertainty quantification and scientific computing.

Alongside his research, he develops data science, machine learning and engineering skills, with an interest in applying these methods to data-intensive problems in research and industry.

Research interests

  • Nuclear reaction theory
  • Superheavy element synthesis
  • Nuclear structure
  • Langevin dynamics
  • Bayesian inference and uncertainty quantification
  • Machine learning for nuclear data
