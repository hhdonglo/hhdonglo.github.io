About

Hope Donglo, PhD, is a theoretical nuclear physicist.

Affiliation: Lecturer, Department of Physics, College of Basic and Applied Sciences, University of Ghana, Legon (appointed August 2026).

Hope develops theoretical models of heavy-ion reactions and superheavy element synthesis, employing stochastic methods, uncertainty quantification, and scientific computing to investigate reaction mechanisms and improve predictive nuclear theory.

Main research interests

  • Nuclear reaction theory
  • Superheavy element synthesis
  • Nuclear structure
  • Langevin dynamics
  • Bayesian inference and uncertainty quantification
  • Machine learning for nuclear data

Educational mission

Inquiry-based, student-centred physics instruction that integrates conceptual reasoning, laboratory practice and computational problem-solving, in support of inclusive, research-informed STEM education.
