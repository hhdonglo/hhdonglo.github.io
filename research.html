Research

Research focus

The research develops computational models of heavy-ion reactions to understand and predict the formation of heavy and superheavy nuclei. Stochastic simulation, uncertainty quantification and scientific computing are the methods that support this central aim.

Research areas

Heavy-Ion Reaction Dynamics

Reaction mechanisms are modelled with stochastic simulation, using Langevin dynamics. The approach covers the full pathway from the colliding nuclei to a superheavy element.

Superheavy Element Synthesis

Production cross-sections for superheavy nuclei, including Z = 119 and Z = 120, are predicted with the KEWPIE2 and KEWPIE3 framework. The work connects to experimental studies of synthesis and decay at S3.

Nuclear Reaction Modelling

Fusion-evaporation reactions and the probability of forming a compound nucleus are calculated with Langevin-based stochastic approaches. Earlier work on nuclear structure used collective and mean-field models, for example for argon isotopes.

Uncertainty Quantification

Bayesian optimisation and machine learning are applied to estimate nuclear model parameters and their uncertainties. Competing theoretical models are benchmarked against each other with systematic uncertainty analysis.

Current research projects

KEWPIE3 Development and Validation

Objective: validate and publish KEWPIE3, the new version of the KEWPIE2 code for fusion-evaporation cross-section predictions in superheavy element synthesis.

Key question: how can the calculation of the compound nucleus formation probability be improved with Langevin-based stochastic approaches?

Fusion Dynamics and Fusion-by-Diffusion Modelling

Objective: revisit the fusion dynamics in the fusion-by-diffusion model for fusion-evaporation production cross sections.

Output: manuscript in preparation (see Publications).

Uncertainty Analysis of Nuclear Reaction Models

Objective: constrain the compound nucleus formation probability in superheavy element synthesis through systematic uncertainty analysis.

Output: manuscript in preparation (see Publications).
